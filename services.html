Our Services

From first fitting to final repair — everything Whole Shoes Nduna offers, with typical pricing so there are no surprises in-store.

01 · In-store

Retail & guided fitting

Every visit starts with a free foot measurement — length, width and arch — before we recommend stock. Ranges are grouped below with indicative pricing; exact stock varies by season.

Athletic

Sneakers & trainers

Running, court and lifestyle sneakers, adult and child sizes.

From R699

Formal

Leather & office shoes

Full-grain oxfords, brogues and loafers for work and formal occasions.

From R1,199

Family

School & everyday shoes

Durable school shoes and casual footwear, with a free re-fit as feet grow.

From R449

02 · Workshop

Repair & restoration

Our in-store cobbler, Siyanda Makhosi, services shoes bought with us and elsewhere. Typical turnaround is 3–5 working days.

R

Resoling

Full or half-sole replacement on leather and rubber-soled shoes.

From R320

R

Restitching

Reinforcing loose seams and welts before they fail completely.

From R180

R

Polish & condition

Deep clean, colour restoration and leather conditioning.

From R110

R

Zip & lace repair

Replacement zips, eyelets and laces for boots and sneakers.

From R95

03 · Delivery & collection

Local delivery around Nelspruit

Can't get to the shop? Most in-stock items are hand-delivered around Nelspruit and Mbombela within a day or two, or held for collection at no charge.

A

Nelspruit & Mbombela

Local hand delivery within 1–2 working days, flat rate R45, free over R700.

B

Wider Mpumalanga

3–4 working days via courier partner, flat rate R95.

C

Click & collect

Free, ready within 2 hours during trading hours at Ilanga Mall.
